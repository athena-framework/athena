## [0.4.6] - 2026-10-18

### Added

- Add `ADI::Spec::ContainerTestCase` for testing services that require a container ([#749]) (George Dietrich) <!-- blacksmoke16 -->
- Register the clock and event_dispatcher services, and AEDA::AsEventListener listeners, when the Clock and Event Dispatcher components are present ([#757]) (George Dietrich) <!-- blacksmoke16 -->

### Fixed

- Allow an `ADI::AsAlias` annotation to replace an alias defined by a bundle extension ([#754]) (George Dietrich) <!-- blacksmoke16 -->
- Fix an infinite recursion error when a service is being autowired with itself ([#757]) (George Dietrich) <!-- blacksmoke16 -->
